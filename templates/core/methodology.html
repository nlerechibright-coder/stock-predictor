System Architecture & Methodology

Technical overview of the forecasting engine and data pipeline.

1. Data Pipeline & Ingestion

The system ingests daily historical market data for Apple Inc. (AAPL) via the yfinance API. The dataset encompasses Open, High, Low, Close, and Volume metrics over a 5-year rolling window.

  • Validation: Automated checks remove missing values, adjust for stock splits, and eliminate duplicate timestamps.
  • Temporal Integrity: Data is strictly ordered chronologically to prevent look-ahead bias and temporal data leakage.

2. Quantitative Feature Engineering

Raw market data is transformed into technical indicators to capture market momentum, volatility, and trend direction:

Moving Averages (MA_7, MA_30)

Smooths price action to identify macro and micro trend directions.

Daily Returns & Volatility

Captures the percentage change and standard deviation of risk.

Autoregressive Lags (Lag_1 to Lag_3)

Previous closing prices used as sequential inputs for time-series forecasting.

Z-Score Standardization

Normalizes all features to a mean of 0 and variance of 1 for model stability.

3. Machine Learning Engine

The core forecasting engine utilizes an XGBoost Regressor (Extreme Gradient Boosting). XGBoost is deployed for its superior ability to model non-linear relationships and complex interactions within tabular financial data.

  • Training Protocol: 80/20 chronological split. No random shuffling is used to maintain temporal sequence.
  • Evaluation Metrics: Mean Absolute Error (MAE), Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE), and Directional Accuracy.
  • Inference Strategy: The model executes autoregressive multi-step forecasting, predicting Day 1, appending the output to the feature set, and iterating to Day 5.
